Frequently Asked Questions

What's free, and what does the Full Library unlock?

Free forever: the complete 66-book King James Bible, the complete Book of Enoch (all 108 chapters), the Canon Map, and full-text search. The one-time Full Library purchase adds 19 more books (the KJV Apocrypha, Jubilees, 1–3 Meqabyan and the Book of Jasher), all guided reading plans, and Markdown export of your highlights and notes. There is no subscription.

Does the app really have all 88 books?

No app or book honestly can — several books of the official 81-entry Tewahedo canon have never been published in English. The Canon Map inside the app shows exactly which books exist in English, which are partial, and which remain untranslated, with the source of every translation. That honesty is the point of the app.

Where do the texts come from?

Every book names its translator, year, source and license on its provenance card, and the full list is in Settings → Licenses & Sources. Highlights include the KJV (ebible.org), R. H. Charles's Enoch (1917) and Jubilees (1902), the 1840 Book of Jasher, and the Wikisource community translations of 1–3 Meqabyan (CC BY-SA 4.0).

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Settings → Restore Purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account you used for the original purchase.

Does the app work offline?

Yes — completely. Every text ships inside the app; nothing is downloaded or streamed.

How do highlights and bookmarks work?

Long-press any verse in the reader to highlight it (four colors), bookmark it, or copy it with its reference. Everything you save appears under Library → the bookmark icon, and can be exported as Markdown with the Full Library unlock.
