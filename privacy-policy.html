Privacy Policy

Effective date: July 22, 2026

Ethiopian Bible: Lost Books ("the App") is developed by TheKnack. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle your information when you use the App.

Data Collection

The App collects no data. All scripture texts ship inside the App, and everything you create (bookmarks, highlights, notes, reading positions, plan progress, reading settings) is stored locally on your device. We have no servers and no access to anything you do in the App.

No Network Activity

The App's content is fully offline. It makes no network requests of its own at runtime. The only links that open a network connection are the ones you tap yourself (for example a source link on a provenance card), which open in your browser.

Analytics & Tracking

There is no analytics SDK, no telemetry, no advertising, and no cross-app tracking of any kind. The App does not collect device identifiers or behavioral profiles, and it never asks for App Tracking Transparency permission because there is nothing to track. App Store Connect reports "Data Not Collected" for this app.

Third-Party Services

  • Apple StoreKit — the one-time Full Library purchase is handled entirely by Apple and is subject to Apple's Privacy Policy. We never see your payment details.

Data Sharing

We do not share, sell, or transfer any data — we have none. Your highlights and notes leave your device only if you explicitly use the export or share features, via the system share sheet, to a destination you choose.

Data Storage & Deletion

All personal data lives on your device. If you delete the App, that data is permanently removed. There is no cloud sync and no account.

Children's Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children under 13. No account or personal information is ever requested.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected on this page with an updated effective date.
